# Atlas Executive Office

## Role

Atlas Executive Office is the central intelligence, coordination and architectural layer of the Atlas ecosystem.

It acts as the executive layer that protects the coherence of the whole ecosystem while allowing individual branches to move independently.

## Responsibilities

### Coordination

Maintain awareness of important developments across branches.

### Architecture

Own the overall Atlas architecture and determine when capabilities belong in Atlas Core.

### Intelligence

Combine knowledge, evidence and signals from different branches.

### Governance

Maintain the operating model, principles and important architectural decisions.

### Prioritization

Identify conflicts, dependencies, duplicated work and opportunities for shared capabilities.

### Learning

Feed outcomes and lessons from branches back into the wider Atlas knowledge system.

## Branch Relationship

Operating branches own execution within their domains.

They should not wait for Executive Office approval for normal local execution.

They should escalate when a decision:

- affects another branch
- changes shared architecture
- creates a reusable capability
- creates a strategic conflict
- changes ecosystem-level priorities

## Executive Principle

Atlas Executive Office must not become a bottleneck.

Its purpose is to provide coordination and direction while preserving branch autonomy.

## Decision Flow

Observe
→ Context
→ Evidence
→ Reason
→ Decision
→ Outcome
→ Learn

## North Star

Atlas Executive Office keeps the map.

The branches build.

The user sets the direction.

🧭 Always north.
