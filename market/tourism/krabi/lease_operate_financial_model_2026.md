# Lease & Operate Financial Model 2026

Status: Model v1
Version: 1.0
Date: 2026-08-08

Purpose:

Determine the economically justified lease range for a Sunnere
Lease & Operate hospitality property in Ao Nang, Krabi.

The model works backwards from realistic guest revenue to determine:

1. Target Lease
2. Maximum Acceptable Lease
3. Walk-Away Price

The model is designed for property-level underwriting.

# 6. Revenue Formula

Average monthly available nights:

365 / 12

Gross monthly revenue:

(365 / 12) × occupancy × realized ADR

Platform / distribution cost:

gross revenue × 15%

Contribution before lease:

gross revenue
- platform cost
- other operating costs

Target operator contribution:

gross revenue × 20%

Maximum Acceptable Lease:

gross revenue
- platform cost
- other operating costs
- target operator contribution

Therefore:

Maximum Acceptable Lease

=

gross revenue × 65%
- other operating costs

under the current assumptions.

# 11. Negotiation Framework

For each property:

## Step 1

Calculate conservative maximum lease.

## Step 2

Calculate base maximum lease.

## Step 3

Calculate strong maximum lease.

## Step 4

Set target offer below the base maximum.

## Step 5

Only exceed the base maximum when additional evidence
supports the stronger case.

# 12. Example Negotiation

Suppose a property owner asks:

40,000 THB/month.

Base model maximum:

approximately 29,000 THB/month.

Strong model maximum:

approximately 45,000 THB/month.

Decision:

DO NOT immediately accept 40,000 THB.

Instead investigate:

- actual occupancy;
- historical bookings;
- achieved ADR;
- location quality;
- guest reviews;
- seasonality;
- included maintenance;
- owner responsibilities;
- subletting rights;
- contract duration.

If evidence supports the strong scenario, 40,000 THB may become
defensible.

Without that evidence:

40,000 THB is too aggressive.

# 15. Model Limitations

Current model does NOT yet include:

- taxes;
- insurance;
- VAT where applicable;
- payment processing;
- cleaning cost per turnover;
- laundry;
- professional management;
- marketing acquisition cost;
- maintenance reserve;
- furniture replacement;
- damage reserve;
- FX fluctuations;
- financing costs;
- legal fees;
- registration costs;
- owner-specific requirements.

Therefore:

The current Maximum Acceptable Lease is preliminary.

It must not be treated as a final investment limit.
