# 5. Pool-Villa Signal

The more relevant segment for Sunnere is likely:

2–3 bedrooms
Private pool
Fully furnished
Guest-ready
Ao Nang / surrounding areas
Short-to-medium lease availability

Observed asking prices in this segment are broadly around:

38,000–65,000 THB/month

with significant variation.

---

# 6. Six-Month Lease Signal

Current evidence shows three different patterns:

Pattern A:

3–6 month contracts available around 30,000 THB/month.

Pattern B:

3–6 month contracts available around 65,000 THB/month.

Pattern C:

6-month contracts can be priced significantly ABOVE
12-month contracts.

Conclusion:

Lease duration must be negotiated property-by-property.
