# 1. Executive Evidence Summary

Krabi shows a substantial tourism and accommodation market.

The current evidence supports continuing investigation into an
asset-light hospitality model.

The evidence does NOT yet prove that a specific leased property will
be profitable.

The next stage must therefore move from market-level evidence to
property-level comparable evidence.

# 5. Rental Market Observation

Current observed Ao Nang listings show a wide price distribution.

This confirms that property-level characteristics matter significantly.

Relevant variables include:

- bedroom count
- pool
- land area
- location
- distance to beach
- property age
- furnishing
- views
- guest capacity
- contract duration
- included services

Therefore:

A province-wide ADR or occupancy figure must NOT be applied directly
to an individual property without comparable-property analysis.

---

# 6. Six-Month Lease Hypothesis

Initial hypothesis:

A six-month lease may provide a useful balance between:

- lower landlord commitment risk;
- sufficient operating time;
- ability to negotiate below short-term pricing;
- limited capital exposure.

However:

Current evidence shows that six-month pricing can be either lower
or higher than longer contracts.

Therefore:

"Six months = cheaper"

is NOT a valid assumption.

The actual contract must be negotiated property by property.

# 7. Legal Evidence

VERIFIED

The Thai Hotel Act defines a hotel as accommodation established for
business purposes providing temporary accommodation for compensation.

The Act excludes accommodation established for accommodation services
with a monthly paid service charge or upward.

Source:

Department of Provincial Administration
Hotel Act translation

Important:

This does NOT by itself establish that every proposed Sunnere
operating model is legally permitted.

Before operating short-term stays, Sunnere must obtain qualified local
legal confirmation covering:

- property classification;
- lease rights;
- subletting;
- guest accommodation;
- minimum stay;
- hotel licence requirements;
- applicable exemptions;
- immigration reporting / TM30;
- platform listing;
- local authority requirements.

Classification:

LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

# 9. Correct Scenario Formula

Monthly available nights:

365 / 12

Gross revenue:

(365 / 12) × occupancy × ADR

Platform cost:

gross revenue × platform percentage

Operating contribution:

gross revenue
- platform cost
- lease
- operating costs
