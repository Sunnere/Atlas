# Krabi Tourism Evidence

Purpose:

Document verified evidence for tourism demand, accommodation demand,
short-term rental performance, rental pricing and operating conditions
in Krabi, with initial focus on Ao Nang.

Evidence classification:

- VERIFIED = directly supported by a documented source
- DERIVED = calculated from verified source data
- ASSUMPTION = internal modelling assumption
- UNKNOWN = not yet sufficiently verified

Rule:

No investment conclusion may be based on an ASSUMPTION when relevant
VERIFIED evidence can reasonably be obtained.

Primary research areas:

- Tourism demand
- Hotel occupancy
- Short-term rental performance
- Seasonality
- Accommodation pricing
- Long-term rental market
- Property supply
- Regulation
- Guest segments
- Operating costs
